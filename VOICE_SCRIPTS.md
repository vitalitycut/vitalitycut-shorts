# Voiceover scripts

Record any script below on your phone (Voice Memos is fine), then upload it to the `voice/` folder named with its slug, e.g. `morning-light.m4a`. The autopilot turns each recording into a Short on its next run. Newest scripts are at the top.

Tips: record in a quiet room with the phone about 6 inches from your mouth. Talk like you're telling a friend, a little faster and more energetic than normal. Retakes are fine; only upload the take you like.

---

## cyclic-sighing
**Topic:** A 5-minute breathing exercise for stress · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Stressed? Try this five-minute breathing trick. Breathe in through your nose, add a second short sip of air, then exhale slowly through your mouth. In a Stanford study, five minutes a day for a month boosted mood a bit more than mindfulness. It was small and in healthy adults, so it's no cure. Try it tonight, and follow for more.

**Sources:** Spiegel, Huberman et al. (Stanford Medicine), Cell Reports Medicine, Jan 2023 (111 healthy adults, 1 month), via Stanford Medicine news and Greater Good Science Center (UC Berkeley).

## light-weights
**Topic:** Light weights can build muscle · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Think you need heavy weights to build muscle? Not necessarily. Research suggests lighter weights can build similar muscle, as long as you push each set close to the point where you can't do another rep. That effort is what does the work. Not a shortcut, though: those last few reps should feel really hard. Follow for more.

**Sources:** Newcastle University HNERC blog (Lim, Sept 2024), citing Schoenfeld et al. 2017 and Carvalho et al. 2022 meta-analyses; Christopher Hurst (Newcastle University) in The Conversation, republished by ScienceDaily, Oct 2026.

## morning-light
**Topic:** Morning daylight and sleep · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Can't fall asleep at night? Try fixing your mornings. Getting outside in daylight soon after you wake up helps set your body clock, so you feel sleepy at the right time later. Even a few minutes counts, and longer on cloudy days. It won't cure insomnia, but it's free. Try it tomorrow and follow for more.

**Sources:** Research on light and the circadian system (e.g. Blume et al., 2019, "Effects of light on human circadian rhythms, sleep and mood," Somnologie); Sleep Foundation guidance on light exposure.
