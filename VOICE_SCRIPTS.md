# Voiceover scripts

Record any script below on your phone (Voice Memos is fine), then upload it to the `voice/` folder named with its slug, e.g. `morning-light.m4a`. The autopilot turns each recording into a Short on its next run. Newest scripts are at the top.

Tips: record in a quiet room with the phone about 6 inches from your mouth. Talk like you're telling a friend, a little faster and more energetic than normal. Retakes are fine; only upload the take you like.

## oatmeal-two-days
**Topic:** The University of Bonn two-day oatmeal trial and what it really tested · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Heard that two days of oatmeal can lower your cholesterol? In a German trial, adults with metabolic syndrome ate three hundred grams of oats a day for two days, on about half their usual calories. Their LDL dropped about ten percent. It was a small study, so it's not a replacement for medication, and talk to your doctor before any crash diet. Follow for more.

**Sources:** Klümpen, Simon et al., "Cholesterol-lowering effects of oats induced by microbially produced phenolic metabolites in metabolic syndrome: a randomized controlled trial," Nature Communications 2026 (DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-68303-9), via News-Medical (Jan 23, 2026) and ScienceDaily (Feb 25, 2026).

## everyday-oat-bowl
**Topic:** Why a normal daily bowl of oats did much less than the headline diet · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Your daily bowl of oats is great, but it's not magic. In the same Bonn study, people who just added eighty grams of oats a day for six weeks saw only modest changes. The big drop came from a short, intense oat diet with fewer calories. Researchers think gut bacteria play a part, but that's still early. Follow for more.

**Sources:** Nature Communications 2026 (University of Bonn, Klümpen et al.), via News-Medical (Jan 23, 2026) and ScienceDaily (Feb 25, 2026).

## bones-like-surprises
**Topic:** Why fast, multi-direction sports beat steady exercise on bone-building blood markers (Semmelweis meta-analysis) · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Your bones might like surprises. A review of twenty-four studies found that sports full of sprints, jumps and quick turns, like soccer and handball, raised bone-building blood markers by up to forty-six percent. **Sources:** Barna, Sydó, Ferdinandy et al., "High-Intensity Training Increases Osteocalcin Levels," Sports Medicine – Open 2026 (DOI 10.1186/s40798-026-00986-2), via Semmelweis University news (Aug 3, 2026) and ScienceDaily (Nov 11, 2026).

## lifting-minutes
**Topic:** How much strength training a week was linked to living longer, from a Harvard study · **Length:** about 20 seconds

A Harvard study of nearly a hundred and fifty thousand adults found that ninety minutes to two hours of strength training a week was linked to a thirteen percent lower risk of early death, with no extra benefit past two hours. ## walking-counts

## sleep-before-training

## cannabis-insomnia
**Topic:** Does medical cannabis help insomnia? What a Cochrane review found · **Length:** about 20 seconds

> Does cannabis actually help you sleep? A new Cochrane review pooled nine trials with about five hundred adults with insomnia. Compared with a placebo, medical cannabis made little or no real difference to sleep quality or total sleep, and side effects like dizziness and dry mouth were more common. The trials were small, so it isn't the final word. Follow for more.

**Sources:** Varela, Garegnani et al., "Medical cannabis for the treatment of insomnia," Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2026, Issue 10 (DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD016216.pub2), via ScienceDaily (Nov 1, 2026).
