## Channel description
> Health studies, minus the hype, in about 30 seconds.
>
> VitalityCut turns new research on food, sleep, fitness, women's health and wellness trends into short, plain-English videos. Every video names its sources and includes a reality check: what the study actually shows, and what it doesn't.
>
> Series:
> • Myth or Fact: popular health rules, put to the test
> • Food on Trial: what the research says about everyday foods and drinks
> • Body Clock: sleep, timing and daily routines
> • Stronger for Longer: strength and longevity habits
> • Her Health: women's health research, without the hype
> • Hype Check: viral trends and supplements, checked against the evidence
>
> New Shorts every day. Subscribe for the next one.
>
> Educational content only, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before changing your diet, exercise or medication.
