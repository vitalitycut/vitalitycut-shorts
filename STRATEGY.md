## Do more of
- Topics with built-in curiosity and a clear audience: women's health, supplements people are already buying, everyday products that might be harmful.
- Hooks that challenge a belief in 8 words or fewer ("X isn't just for Y", "Your ___ might be ___").

## Do less of
- Nothing yet.

## Format mix to test (next 2 weeks)
Rotate so no two videos in a row share a format: myth vs. fact, "3 things", "X vs Y", "what happens when", quick explainer.

## Keep it monetizable
Since July 16, 2026, YouTube doesn't monetize repetitive or templated content with little variation between videos. Every video must look and read different from the last one:
- Change the palette, particle style, label wording and beat structure each time.
- Vary length between about 25 and 45 seconds.
- Make each one teach something specific; never pad with generic lines.
